# Example Corp — Paid Time Off Policy

> Synthetic document for a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) lab. Example Corp is fictional.

**Policy owner:** People Operations · **Effective:** 2026-01-01 · **Version:** 1.4

## Annual leave
- Full-time employees accrue **20 days** of paid annual leave per year, rising to **25 days** after five years of service.
- Up to **5 unused days** carry over into the next calendar year; carried-over days expire on **March 31**.
- Requests longer than **5 consecutive working days** need at least **4 weeks'** notice.

## Sick leave
- Employees receive **10 days** of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than **3 consecutive days**.

## Company holidays
- Example Corp observes **11 company holidays** per year, published each December for the following year.

## Parental leave
- Birth and non-birth parents receive **16 weeks** of fully paid parental leave, to be taken within 12 months of the child's arrival.
