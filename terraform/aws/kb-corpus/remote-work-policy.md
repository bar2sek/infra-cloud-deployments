# Example Corp — Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

> Synthetic document for a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) lab. Example Corp is fictional.

**Policy owner:** People Operations · **Effective:** 2026-03-01 · **Version:** 2.0

## Eligibility
- Employees become eligible for hybrid work after completing a **90-day** onboarding period.
- Hybrid employees work from an Example Corp office at least **2 days per week**; Tuesday is the company-wide in-office anchor day.
- Fully remote arrangements require approval from both the department head and People Operations, and are reviewed **annually**.

## Home office stipend
- Eligible employees receive a one-time home office stipend of **$600**, plus **$50 per month** toward internet costs.
- Equipment bought with the stipend belongs to the employee; company laptops and phones remain Example Corp property.

## Working from abroad
- Employees may work from another country for up to **20 working days** in any 12-month period, with manager approval.
- Longer stays require a tax and immigration review, requested at least **30 days** in advance.

## Core hours
- Core collaboration hours are **10:00–15:00** in the employee's home office time zone.
