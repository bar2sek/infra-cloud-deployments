# Example Corp — Travel & Expense Policy

> Synthetic document for a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) lab. Example Corp is fictional.

**Policy owner:** Finance Operations · **Effective:** 2026-01-01 · **Version:** 3.2

## Submitting expenses
- Submit every expense report within **30 calendar days** of the purchase date. Reports older than 60 days are rejected unless the CFO approves an exception.
- Attach an itemised receipt for any single expense of **$25 or more**.
- Expenses above **$500** require approval from your direct manager *before* purchase.

## Travel
- Book flights through the corporate travel portal at least **14 days** before departure when possible.
- Economy class is standard. Premium economy is allowed on flights longer than **6 hours**.
- Hotel nightly limit: **$250** in standard cities and **$350** in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich).

## Meals
- Daily meal allowance while travelling: **$75** per day, receipts required.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at client entertainment events approved in advance by a vice president.

## Reimbursement
- Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle, normally within **10 business days**.
